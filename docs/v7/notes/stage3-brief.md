## Build
1. `Assets/Scripts/StationTarget.ts` (self-contained, built in code, no `@input`): one target per station (legacy dishes: SkilletStation, SaucepotStation, PrepBoardStation and the plate; Cook-engine dishes: one per vessel/board/plate in use, fed from the Cook runtime/StepCard content). UIKit circular button (or the project's SIK Interactable button if UIKit cannot be created from code, say why), collider >= 4.5 cm, 8 cm above the vessel rim on the cook's side, registered with `HeadPoseBillboard` (yaw only). Three states: DOT (1.2 cm visual at 40% brightness, 4.5 cm pinch collider stays), OPEN (12 x 7 cm dark glass backplate: step title 1.0 cm cap height, timer 1.8 cm tabular numerals, one-line hint max four words at 0.8 cm, the 4.5 cm circle becomes the Done button), ALERT (OPEN plus coral accent pulsing 1 Hz; the chime comes in Stage 4). Open/close rules now: step start, 10 s before the timer ends, drift/heat events, gaze within 12 deg for 1 s, pinching the dot toggles; close 5 s after the last trigger with no alert. Subscribe now to HAND_AT_STATION / HAND_LEFT_STATION / ALERT_RAISED / ALERT_RESOLVED (they fire from Stage 4). Pinching Done emits STEP_DONE_PINCHED and advances through the existing StepEngine/DAG path (single source of truth). Microcopy: verbs first, four words max.
2. The plate target's OPEN card has two actions: "Plate" and "Name a dish" (emits OPEN_DISH_STUDIO and opens Dish Studio exactly as the old AI Dish button did). A 1.5 s hold-pinch on the plate target emits PALETTE_REQUESTED.
3. `Assets/Scripts/DirectorPalette.ts`: hidden palette replacing the dock. Holds the SIM/debug controls (+25 s drift, 10x warp, Reset, Auto Cook/rehearsal, AI vision cycle, AI Dish quick-cycle, Steps chip) plus Claim kitchen (`ClaimController.begin`), Quick claim (`ClaimController.quickClaim`), Forget kitchen (`KitchenPersistence.forget()` then `KitchenAnchorRoot.resetPose()`), Show ghosts (flag only; Stage 7 wires it). Opens on PALETTE_REQUESTED and key D in the editor; keys 1-0 keep working. Existing dock `Btn_*` objects keep their names and `MiseFlowButtonTrigger` actions; with the palette closed no dock/palette object is visible.
4. Pills, dock capsule and legacy step cards are hidden (not destroyed, not renamed) in the product for dishes 01-06 and Cook dishes, so scenario lookups and text asserts (e.g. `SkilletStationUI_Action`) keep working. Dish 01, the not-found string and the 0.55 gate are untouched.
5. Scenarios (new files in `Assets/Tests`, registered in `LeafIndex.ts`): `single-ui-invariant-scenario` (dish 02, then Chicken 65: each produces station targets, no dock/palette object visible, no legacy pill visible; leave a marked TODO for the Stage 5 rail assertion), `fov-budget-scenario` (steady state: visible UI within a 50 x 35 x 20 cm band around the kitchen root, nothing parented under `Camera Object`, target colliders >= 4.5 cm), and update `station-setup-scenario` to also assert a StationTarget per legacy station.
