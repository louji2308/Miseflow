# Stage 4 brief: hands as the sensor + attention director (chime and arrow)

Lens project: `Miseflow/` (Lens Studio 5.24, TypeScript). Units cm, right-handed, camera faces -Z, additive display. Read `UPGRADE_PLAN_V7.md` sections A, D5, D6, D10, S4, S9 (device hands), E3, E4, Part F rows hand-zone-target-scenario / attention-chime-beacon-scenario, Part G stage 4; the tracker in `todo.md` (decisions D-001..D-014, Hard invariants); Stage 1-3 sources: `KitchenAnchorRoot.ts`, `HeadPoseBillboard.ts` (has `setSimulatedCameraWorldPos` and `cameraWorldPos`), `ClaimController.ts` (Station_* objects when claimed), `StationTarget.ts` (already subscribes to HAND_AT_STATION / HAND_LEFT_STATION / ALERT_RAISED / ALERT_RESOLVED), `DirectorPalette.ts`, `GazeEdgeBeacon.ts` (has `setSimulatedCameraYaw`), `MiseFlowEventBus.ts`, `Assets/Tests/MiseFlowLeafInteractor.ts` and an existing hand-driven scenario.

## Build
1. `Assets/Scripts/HandZoneSensor.ts` (self-contained, built in code, no `@input`, created from `MiseFlowSpatialUI.ensureHeroComponents` next to the other ensure() calls): assigns each tracked hand to the nearest station (claimed `Station_*` objects, otherwise the legacy SkilletStation / SaucepotStation / PrepBoardStation / plate anchor) within 25 cm and keeps a dwell timer per station. Arrival emits HAND_AT_STATION {station, hand}, leaving emits HAND_LEFT_STATION. Public: `getHandStation(hand)`, `getDwellSeconds(stationId)`, `isHandAt(stationId)`, `setSimulatedHand(worldPos | null)` for LEAF. Device path (guarded by `global.deviceInfoSystem.isEditor()`): SIK `HandInputData` (indexTip, wrist), subscriptions inside `OnStartEvent`, labelled "feel unproven". Editor path: the active SIK interactor's hit/pinch point (raw hand events do not fire in the editor) and the LEAF puppet hand. Visible fallback everywhere: pinching a StationTarget means "I am here" (emit HAND_AT_STATION for that station).
2. `Assets/Scripts/AttentionDirector.ts` (same creation pattern): one alert at a time; priority order: heat guard, then a step starting at an off-view station, then a timer under 10 s. Raises ALERT_RAISED {station, kind} and ALERT_RESOLVED. A 0.4 s two-tone chime spatialised at the station position, repeating every 8 s while unresolved, never more than one chime per 8 s. Resolved when gaze is within 15 deg of the station, a hand arrives (HAND_AT_STATION) or Done is pinched (STEP_DONE_PINCHED). Drives `GazeEdgeBeacon` (it points to the alerted station); keep the beacon's existing scenario behaviour intact. Public: `getActiveAlert()`, `getChimeCount()`, `getLastChimeTime()` for LEAF, and a `raiseTestAlert(stationId, kind)` hook.
3. Chime asset: a license-clean two-tone chime generated locally (skills ls-clad:build-sfx and ls-clad:specs-audio; Lens Studio reports it is not signed in, so if the generative tool refuses, synthesize the audio yourself: write a 16-bit PCM WAV (about 0.4 s, 660 Hz then 880 Hz with a short fade) with PowerShell/.NET or Node, import it through the proper tool (skill ls-clad:editor-api, `ExecuteEditorCode`) into `Assets/Audio/`, or generate it procedurally at runtime if the d.ts offers a procedural audio provider). No third-party audio. Spatialize with the AudioComponent's spatial settings at the station. No music.
4. Scenarios (new files in `Assets/Tests`, registered in `LeafIndex.ts`): `hand-zone-target-scenario` (a puppet pinch-hold or `setSimulatedHand` 10 cm from the board, then move away: the board target opens within 0.5 s, and collapses 5 s after leaving) and `attention-chime-beacon-scenario` (simulated camera yaw 40 deg via the existing simulated-camera hooks, raise a skillet alert: the beacon is visible, the chime plays exactly once, repeats after 8 s (use the time-warp or a shortened test interval hook if needed, say which), and the alert resolves when turning back).

## Hard rules
SIK/hand/gesture subscriptions inside `OnStartEvent`; hit-test/camera sessions started in `OnStartEvent`; branch device vs editor only through `global.deviceInfoSystem.isEditor()`; never rename existing objects or change LEAF-asserted names/strings; one alert at a time; one chime per 8 s; dish 01 and the not-found string untouched; nothing parented to the camera.
